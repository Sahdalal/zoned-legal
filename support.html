Common questions

My zone didn't start when I arrived.

Open Settings → Zones & permissions → Zone status in Zoned. It lists everything that could stop a zone from starting. The usual cause is location set to "While Using" instead of "Always". Without "Always", iOS can't tell Zoned you've arrived while the app is closed.

Apps aren't being blocked.

Blocking uses Apple's Screen Time framework. It needs your one-time permission (iOS asks the first time you pick apps to block), and it needs apps picked for that zone. Zone status shows whether both are in place.

Does Zoned see my Screen Time or which apps I use?

No. Apple keeps both on your device and gives them to no app. See the privacy policy for exactly what Zoned does collect.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account & data → Delete account. It removes your account and all of its data, right away.

I forgot my accountability PIN.

The PIN exists only on your phone, so we can't see or reset it. That's what makes it work. Email us and we'll help.

Accessibility

We want Zoned to work for everyone. It's built to work with VoiceOver and the system text size, and it offers light and dark appearances. We're still checking every screen against these, so if something is hard to use with VoiceOver, large text, or any assistive technology, please email us. Accessibility reports get priority.
