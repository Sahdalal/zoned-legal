Zoned

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Zoned is operated by SDP LLC

Zoned lets you save the places in your life, choose apps to block at each one, and have that block start when you arrive. This policy says what that takes, where it goes, and how to make it go away. If anything here is unclear, write to realzonedapp@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

Location

Zoned uses your location on your phone to tell whether you are inside one of your zones. With "Always" permission, iOS itself watches your zones' boundaries in the background. Your moving position is not uploaded or stored on our servers. What is stored is the coordinates of the zones you saved, and the sessions described above, which reveal that you were at one of your zones at a given time. You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Zoned. Zones won't start on their own while it's off.

What we do not collect

  • Your Screen Time or app usage. Apple does not give those numbers to any app, and Zoned never sees them.
  • Which apps you block, or which apps you have installed. You pick apps in Apple's own picker, and Apple keeps those choices on your device. Zoned receives only opaque tokens that stay on the phone.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data.
  • Advertising identifiers. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Some things stay on your device and are never sent to us: your accountability PIN (kept in the iOS Keychain), the apps you picked to block, and the error log in Settings → Help → Diagnostics.

Who else processes your data

We do not sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising. We use these services to run Zoned:

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. It stores the account, profile, zone, session and progress data above. Access is limited so that each account can read and write only its own rows.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, location services, notifications, and the Screen Time framework that shields apps.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim), run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation, receives the text you type into place search, along with your IP address, so it can return matching places. No account information is sent with it.

We choose providers that protect your data at least as well as this policy does, and we give them only what their part of the service needs. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Sessions (when a block ran at one of your zones) are kept for twelve months, then deleted. Before they're deleted, we keep only the totals your progress is built from: how many weeks you were active and how many blocks you saw through. Your star, planets and journeys stay exactly where they were. We retain everything else (your account, zones and progress) for as long as your account exists, so it's there when you come back. When you delete your account, everything connected to it is deleted from our database, and that can't be undone. Copies may remain for a short time in our hosting provider's encrypted backups until those backups expire.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in Zoned, go to Settings → Account & data → Delete account. This removes your account and all of its data. You don't need to email us.
  • Get a copy of your data: in Zoned, go to Settings → Account & data → Export my data. It saves everything your account holds as a file. To correct something, email us from your account's address and we'll respond within 30 days.
  • Withdraw permissions: location and notifications can be turned off in iOS Settings at any time. If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also remove Zoned in iOS Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.
  • Sign out: this clears Zoned's data from the phone. It stays in your account until you delete it.

Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU or UK), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a regulator. We honor these requests through the same email address.

Children

Zoned is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels to our servers encrypted (HTTPS). Database access is restricted per account, and sensitive values on your phone, such as your PIN, are kept in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach ever affected your data, we would tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If the change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
